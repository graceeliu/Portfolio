Content design across two surfaces at Workiva: the customer help center, and content for the governance, risk, and compliance side of the product.

Documentation is a category of writing where the reader never wants to be there. Someone opening a help article is already blocked, already mildly annoyed, and looking for one specific thing. That changes what good writing means — the goal isn't to be read, it's to get someone unblocked and gone. Structure, findability, and knowing which step people actually get stuck on matter more than prose does.

GRC content has close to the opposite constraint. The subject matter is complex and consequential, the audience is expert, and precision can't be traded away for simplicity. The writing has to be exact without becoming impenetrable.

These are live production pages, and they have been maintained by the team since I worked on them — so the current text may not be mine word for word.
