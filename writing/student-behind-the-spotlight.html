The student behind the spotlight

Category
Journalism
Form
Reported profile
Published
UCSB Humanities & Fine Arts, March 2023

A profile written for the UCSB Humanities & Fine Arts website.

I wanted to spotlight an often overlooked role behind the glamorous theater stage, and it just happens that lighting design is a role that becomes invisible when it's done well. The audience notices a spotlight following an actor and attributes the effect to the performance. So the piece had to make a technical craft legible to readers who had never thought about it, including the division between lighting design and technical lighting.

The student was one of very few lighting students in the department, which meant unusual access to large productions as an undergraduate — and the show she had adapted was about to tour five countries.
