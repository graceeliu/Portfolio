Cultural appropriation in social-media fashion trends

Category
Journalism
Form
Reported magazine feature
Context
Magazine writing course, UC Santa Barbara
Full paper
PDF

When Hailey Bieber posted her “brownie-glazed lips,” brown liner with a glossy lip, the video got millions of views. Black and Latina women pointed out that they’d been wearing the same look since the ’90s and getting called “ghetto” for it. This feature follows that pattern across TikTok, from “spa water” that’s really agua fresca to filters that put Chola makeup on white faces. Through interviews with UCSB students and influencers, it asks why a look only becomes a trend once a white creator wears it. It also looks at how beauty filters and recommendation algorithms built around Eurocentric defaults help appropriation spread and fade away quickly with no consequences.
