The debate over AI sentience

Category
Science communication
Form
Essay
Context
Professional writing minor, UC Santa Barbara
Full paper
PDF

As the first writing assignment of my minor, we were instructed to write a short piece that detailed the public vs. scientific perspective of a topic of our choosing.

In a 2023 survey, about half of Americans said they worried AI could cause human extinction, and three in four expected it to become smarter than humans. Much of that fear comes from science fiction, viral robot videos, and a Google engineer who was convinced his chatbot had feelings. AI researchers see it differently. They treat superintelligence as possible but not imminent, and they’re more worried about harms that are already here: misinformation, surveillance, and systems that absorb bias from their training data and amplify it. My paper compares the public and scientific views and argues that fear of a machine takeover pulls attention away from the problems AI is causing now.

I wrote this as a psychology student with a growing interest in AI from a humanities perspective. I now work on how people form trust in an AI system that talks to them, and the essay's central move — separate the speculative risk from the deployed one, and take the deployed one more seriously — is roughly how I still think about it.
